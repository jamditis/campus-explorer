    About this model

    Source snapshot: loading…

    This is a work in progress, not a survey or an as-built record. Building detail, ground grading and geographic alignment remain provisional, and many parts are marked unverified in the source model.

    The campus is shown as one scene. It is still modelled in two Blender files that overlap in the middle; the viewer joins their 160 m tiles, and where both files cover a tile only one is shown. Seams at those tile edges are unverified until the files are merged.

    The browser edition simplifies the Blender model: materials become the model’s own colours over tileable CC0 textures mapped in world space, lot markings are painted from the model’s own line work, trees are thinned, distant scenery is lighter, small details fade out with distance, and lawn grass blades and some interiors are left out.

    Sunlight uses the real position of the sun over the campus for the chosen date and time. Latitude, longitude and elevation come from the model’s New Jersey State Plane frame and NJ LiDAR as modelled. Measurements are taken on the model and are not survey values.

    Place names and their positions are from OpenStreetMap. Walk mode follows the modelled ground and stops at solid walls. Trees and small details do not block you.

    Selected surface and leaf textures are from ambientCG, licensed CC0; other leaf atlases are authored for this project. Reference photographs and aerial imagery are not redistributed. Elevation comes from public NJ/USGS LiDAR; some layout follows OpenStreetMap.
