# Teaser soundtrack: credits and licences

Every sound in the teaser's soundtrack is a recording made by a person with a microphone. Nothing is
synthesised, generated or spoken by a machine. The mix only cuts, filters, fades, pans and sums the
recordings (`mix.py`, `cues.json`).

Each licence below was checked on the file's own page on 2026-11-21, the day the files were fetched.
Keys are the names used in `cues.json` and the file names in the source folder.

Three recordings are under CC BY 3.0, which requires the credit given for them. Everything else is in
the public domain (CC0, the Public Domain Mark, or a US government work) and needs no credit, but is
credited anyway.

Put this text in the video description, or on the end card, wherever the film is published:

> Sound: field recordings and sound effects from radio aporee ::: maps, the Yellowstone National Park
> Sound Library (National Park Service) and the USC School of Cinematic Arts sound effects libraries,
