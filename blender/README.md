# Blender

- `smoke_test.py` builds a tiny generated scene, checks the straight-run, true-arc and raise-only paving rules on it, renders one small Cycles CPU frame and exports a GLB. CI runs it with Blender 4.5 LTS (`.github/workflows/blender-smoke.yml`).
- `montclair-state/scripts/` is the archive of `bpy` scripts from the Montclair State working sessions, one folder per session (`ws205/`, `ws214/` and so on), plus top-level build and camera scripts such as `build_scene.py`. They were run on the modelling box against the main `.blend`, often exec'd with shared globals, so many will not run standalone and paths point at the box layout (`/workspace/nj-scenes/...`). Treat them as a record of how the scene was built. CI only syntax-checks them.

- `scripted/` holds the scripted builds, one folder per area. `scripted/southwest/` is the southwest corner (Montclair Heights station surroundings, Normal Avenue, Carlisle Road, Sprague Field); `scripted/northeast/` is the north-east district (the parking deck, the residence halls, the Student Recreation Center, the Energy Plant, the softball and tennis fields); `scripted/west/` is Carlisle Road north and the College Avenue junction, between the railway and the Red Hawk Deck; `scripted/village/` is the Village west of the railway (Clove Road, the residence halls and their lots); `scripted/overlook/` is the Overlook Corporate Center block north of the stadium; `scripted/stadium/` is the stadium district north of the north-east area (Yogi Berra Stadium, the museum, the track and soccer field). It makes its own `.blend` from plan files and public evidence, and every run rebuilds it whole. Start with [scripted/README.md](scripted/README.md).

The models themselves are on [Releases](https://github.com/jamditis/campus-explorer/releases), not in git.
